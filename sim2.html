WAIT—THERE IS NO TRADITIONAL "PLOT"

If your teacher asks for the plot, do not make up a story. This is a collection of 44 personal essays adapted from John Green's podcast. The "plot" is the overall arc: John Green rates random parts of our human-centered planet (the Anthropocene) on a 1-to-5 star scale—moving from cynicism and anxiety toward hope and gratitude for being alive.

THE SETUP
Humans reshape Earth; anxiety & isolation.

THE DEEP DIVE
History + personal memories of random objects.
ACT III
THE PAYOFF
Choosing hope & "falling in love with the world."
